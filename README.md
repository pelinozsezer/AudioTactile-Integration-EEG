# AudioTactile-Integration-EEG

## 📌 Project Overview
This project investigated **audiotactile integration** in a **cocktail-party environment (i.e., congruent auditory and tactile stimuli)** using **EEG recordings**. The study aimed to determine whether **congruent audiotactile stimuli** enhanced auditory encoding in the brain and how tactile stimulation influenced auditory perception due to **temporal frequency connections**.

### 🔬 Research Focus
- **Multisensory Integration:** Interaction between auditory and tactile stimuli.
- **EEG Analysis:** Examining how the brain encodes music and vibrations.
- **Real-Life Stimuli:** Use of **monophonic and polyphonic piano music** with synchronized **tactile vibrations**.
- **Neuroscience & Computational Modeling:** Analysis performed with **MATLAB mTRF Toolbox**.

---

## 🛠 Methods & Tools
### **1️⃣ Experiment Design**
- Participants were exposed to **monophonic & polyphonic piano music**.
- Music was paired with **synchronized tactile vibrations**.
- EEG data was collected to examine **neural encoding of auditory and tactile stimuli**.

### **2️⃣ Data Processing & Analysis**
- **EEG Preprocessing:** Filtering, artifact removal, and segmentation.
- **[mTRF Toolbox:](https://www.mtrftoolbox.org)** Used for reconstructing music features (**envelope, pitch**) from EEG signals.
- **Statistical Analysis:** To measure the impact of **tactile congruency** on neural responses.
